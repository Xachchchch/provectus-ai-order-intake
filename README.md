# Junior AI Engineer starter pack

Choose the folder for your assigned task under `tasks/`. Complete one assignment.

Read its `domain.md` before generating more data. These are fictional rules supplied for the exercise. You do not need to research the industry or invent business policies. Preserve the rules and supplied cases; you may adapt file formats or language when the brief allows it, recording the mapping.

Each folder contains sample inputs, expected seed results or reference cases, and templates or a small baseline. Extend these only as needed to cover the assignment. Data preparation remains part of the eight-hour limit; allow about 30–60 minutes. The supplied expected results apply to the original seeds. Check new expected results separately before comparing your application with them.

Read `skills/generate-assignment-data/SKILL.md` with your coding assistant, or copy its instructions into a prompt. Installing a skill is optional. It generates fixtures; it does not implement the assessed application. Use the supplied domain rules if the assistant suggests different ones.

Copy `ai-workflow/` into your submission repository, rename its template files, and complete them. Save the exercise-specific AI configuration you actually used. No custom skill, agent, or hook is mandatory; record `not-used` where appropriate. Keep active configuration in its normal tool location and record that path in the manifest. Sanitize copies of user-level settings rather than copying your entire personal configuration.

All people, businesses, transactions, and policies in this pack are fictional.
